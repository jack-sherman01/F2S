Replaying failures verbatim doesn’t teach recovery

The same fine-tuned checkpoint format, evaluated identically to every other method. Fixed Policy is the frozen, unmodified baseline. Both alternatives make some use of the policy’s own failed rollouts — SOE by exploring near the action manifold at evaluation time, Failure Replay by fine-tuning on raw failed trajectories with plain behavior cloning. Neither beats doing nothing.

Why SOE collapses
CADS-style noise is injected into the diffusion conditioning on every action query, for the full episode, unfiltered. It's built for generating diverse candidates during data collection — not for autonomous deployment as-is.
Why Failure Replay hurts
Plain behavior cloning has no reward signal, so it can't tell this action led to failure from this action led to success. Replaying raw failures teaches the policy to imitate them a little more.
